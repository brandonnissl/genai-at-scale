# PMO & Portfolio Governance

## Cadence
- **Quarterly:** planning and roadmap review across engineering; OKRs tied to programs
- **Biweekly:** executive business review (status, risks, decisions needed)
- **Weekly:** program syncs and RAID log review

## Intake
Every program has a sponsor, a business case, success metrics, and a budget line (tooling, cloud, headcount, vendors).

## Executive report (one page)
| Program | Status | Trend | Top risk | Decision needed | Spend vs. plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Rules
One accountable owner per program. RACI/DACI for cross-team decisions. Postmortems tracked to closure.
